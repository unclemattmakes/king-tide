## Scope

"Game content" means the first-party creative material of King Tide,
wherever it lives:

- everything served from the asset CDN (`public/assets/**`, `public/audio/**`
  at runtime — models, textures, atlases, thumbnails, audio mixes);
- the track designs and gameplay data under `public/tracks/*.json` and
  `specs/**`;
- first-party images and concept material in the repo or its history;
- the raw authoring sources (`.blend` files, concept art) kept outside git.
