## Location & fiction

Drowned Miami Beach. Ocean Drive underwater, Art Deco hotel rooftops
poking through as a chain of pastel islands. Palms still standing on
rooftop "islands" — locals kept them alive. "South Beach kept the lights
on. They held a permanent spring break on the roofs."

## Layout & beats

Asymmetric kidney loop. Reference: Wave Race 64 Sunset Bay (ramp intro) +
Sunny Beach (palette).

| t (s) | Beat | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Atlantic swell straight | Open-water start; pumping pays off, swell rolling shoreward |
| 10–22 | Rooftop chain weave | Three pastel rooftops in series; ride water between, skim each crown |
| 22–32 | **Versace Steps (set-piece)** | Wide-right approach; seaplane-wing ramp launches into next lagoon |
| 32–40 | Inner-bay calm | Gulf-side calmer water; pool-deck shortcut vs longer outer route |
| 40–45 | Finish wraparound | Tight left past the lifeguard station to start |

## Set-piece — Versace Steps

Casa Casuarina's front steps emerge from the water; a half-buried seaplane
sits across them. You launch off the **wing ramp** for big air into the
next lagoon. Visible from the start grid; the half-buried fuselage casts a
long shadow on the steps so players see it at t≈4. Sits at 55–70% of lap
distance — the hard-section slot. Skill: timing the wing-ramp launch with
the incoming swell.

## Hard section / branching / per-lap

- **Hard:** 22–32 s (Versace approach) — wave-timed ramp launch.
- **Branching:** pool-deck shortcut (32–40 beat, ~1.5 s, costs the finish
  line angle); the wing ramp is bypassable on Casual (swim around the
  outside); pro line always takes the ramp.
- **Per-lap:** none. Simplest track in the set; tide constant.

## Palette & audio

Pastel pink / turquoise / neon mint; Art Deco geometry; sunset golden hour.
`miami_pastel` sky. Vaporwave / Miami synth-funk, distant gulls.
