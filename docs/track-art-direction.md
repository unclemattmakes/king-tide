## How to read the ratio

Every track is a deliberate mix of the three material states
([art-direction.md](./art-direction.md)): **Built** (kept/maintained/salvaged,
warm) · **Broken** (flood-taken/oxidised/bleached, cool) · **Blooming**
(reclaimed by life, vivid). The ratio *is* the mood — and all three should be
visible in the hero framing. A pure-broken track is ruin-porn; a pure-blooming
track is a garden centre; a pure-built track forgot the apocalypse. The numbers
below are dressing targets, not law — read them off the lore tag and trust the
place.

## Tutorial — Mayday Bay · Reef pastel · 45 / 15 / 40

A retrofitted post-flood marina serving as the pilot-training cove. **Low-key on
purpose** — every other track is a spectacle; this is a classroom, so dress it
*calm and legible*, never busy.

- **Built (45):** the retrofitted marina — clean repainted dock, the single
  ramp, a maintained pilot-school shack, marker buoys. This is a *cared-for*
  place; lean built.
- **Broken (15):** a light salt-bleach band, one half-sunk piling. Minimal.
- **Blooming (40):** calm-water palms, gentle reef under the glassy cove, a
  little coral on the dune crest.
- **Waterline:** gentle and clean — this is where the player first reads water,
  so the crust/algae band should be *clear and instructive*, not grimy.
- **Set-piece (training gates):** the dune crest the player launches off. Keep
  the read dead-simple — a clean takeoff lip against open sky, no clutter to
  distract from the lesson.

## 1. Mexico City · Reef rosa · 50 / 20 / 30

"They drained the lake to build the city. The lake was patient." A defiantly-
alive drowned megacity — high built + blooming, ruin kept to a budget. Warm
rosa-mexicano-and-marigold light over a teal high-altitude lake. *(Replaced
South Beach Sunken / Miami in the 2026-06 content pass.)*

- **Built (50):** painted colonial facades + Aztec stonework kept lit, the gold
  Ángel, the half-sunk cathedral with gilded altarpiece glints, colourful
  trajineras, papel-picado, the causeway roadways.
- **Broken (20):** the collapsed Segundo Piso freeway deck (the ramp), the
  cathedral's lean, a mineral/salt band on lower facades — keep it a budget.
- **Blooming (30):** jacarandas + ahuehuete cypress locals kept, chinampa reeds
  and floating-garden green, lily/algae fringe at the line, the lake itself.
- **Waterline:** facades + basalt causeway meet teal water with a bright reed/
  lily fringe — keep it *bright*, among the friendliest shorelines in the set
  (the lake is alkaline, so a paler salt-bleach band reads true).
- **Set-piece (El Ángel + collapsed-freeway ramp):** the gold Ángel standing in
  the lake with the fallen Segundo Piso deck as the launch. Make the tilted deck
  read as an obvious takeoff lip; the gold statue + Popocatépetl behind sell the
  postcard.

## 2. Cape Town Drift · Reef pastel (cool grade) · 25 / 50 / 25

A ruin-field harbour — broken-heavy, but "Cape Town's still here, just lower."
Cooler than the other Reef tracks (bright Atlantic blue, mountain grey-green).

- **Built (25):** survivor harbour structures, a few lit windows, the still-
  standing aquarium shell roof.
- **Broken (50):** the dominant note — oxidised container reds, the half-tilted
  Cape Wheel, shipping-yard wreckage, the shattered predator tank.
- **Blooming (25):** harbour kelp, algae on the wreckage, the mountain's living
  grey-green flat top on the skyline.
- **Waterline:** heavy crust + rust-streak on the container/wreck field; this is
  a working ruin, let the oxidation show (still no grimdark — keep colour).
- **Set-piece (Two Oceans Wreck):** the broken aquarium with the great white
  still circling. The shark reads as *blooming-life-persisting*; light the tank
  interior so the silhouette of the shark is unmistakable through the broken roof.

## 3. Hatteras Light · Reef pastel (cool grade) · 30 / 35 / 35

Cool Atlantic greys, the lighthouse a third submerged, the only landmark for
kilometres. Balanced ratio — the lighthouse is *kept*, the sea is *winning*.

- **Built (30):** the black-and-white spiral lighthouse, lamp still spinning
  ("someone keeps the lamp spinning") — maintained, the one warm point.
- **Broken (35):** the submerged third, weathered gallery rail, foam-worn stone.
- **Blooming (35):** foam-green water, gull-perch crags with sea life, kelp at
  the base.
- **Waterline:** the lighthouse base gets the full trio — barnacle crust, salt
  bleach, kelp skirt — it's been standing in rising water for decades.
- **Set-piece (The Lamp Room):** the rotating lamp at the top of the gallery-
  spiral ramp; *the music swells as you reach it*. The lamp is legit emissive —
  a warm rotating beacon against low grey cloud. Big-air off the rail into the
  cliff drop; sell the height with the cool grey sea far below.

## 4. The Maw · Open Sea cool · 5 / 30 / 65

All-ocean, no land contact, golden-hour Pacific. Nature-dominant — barely any
built; this is the purest *world-as-place* track.

- **Built (5):** none, really — maybe a single weathered nav-marker. The bridge
  that fell is *gone*; only the rock stayed.
- **Broken (30):** the collapsed Bixby superstructure chunk, weathered cliff grey.
- **Blooming (65):** the dominant — deep navy Pacific, gold rock arches, white
  foam, kelp, McWay Falls catching afternoon light. The sea *is* the content.
- **Waterline:** every arch base wears kelp + foam-scour + a tide-stain band;
  the rock should read wet and alive at the surface, dry-gold above.
- **Set-piece (The Maw arch):** the largest arch, played by the swell. Light it
  golden-hour so the foam and the wet rock pop; the drama is the wall of water,
  not surface detail. Silhouette the arch hard against bright sky.

## 5. Shibuya Submerged · Urban neon · 60 / 25 / 15

"Tokyo didn't evacuate. They moved up." The most *built* track — Wipeout-bright,
not Akira-rainy. The city as defiant party.

- **Built (60):** lit skyscraper tops, kanji neon, rooftop generators/microgrids,
  narrow rooftop bridges, signage everywhere. This is the neon showcase.
- **Broken (25):** the drowned crossing ten storeys below, the collapsed Cocoon
  lattice, wet asphalt on the rooftop sections.
- **Blooming (15):** sparse rooftop green, a little algae in the flooded streets
  below.
- **Waterline (ten storeys down):** the flooded Shibuya Crossing — neon reflected
  *up* through the water; Hachiko underwater, patient. Light the underwater neon
  reflection as a hero effect.
- **Set-piece (Crossing Cables):** racing across toppled neon signage + powerline
  cables over the submerged scramble. Saturate the hot-pink/electric-blue neon;
  bloom does the work. The reflection in the water below is the postcard.

## 6. Marina Bay 7 · Urban neon (industrial grade) · 50 / 40 / 10

"The port automated itself and nobody told it to stop." Built-but-filthy —
running machinery in an oxidised harbour. Industrial palette (sodium yellow,
steel grey, orange containers) over the neon family's brights.

- **Built (50):** gantry cranes still running on rooftop solar, container stacks,
  sodium-lamp lighting, the automated systems that won't quit.
- **Broken (40):** oxidised hull reds, the beached supertanker, rust streaks,
  murky brown-green harbour water.
- **Blooming (10):** harbour algae, barnacle crust — minimal, it's an industrial
  zone.
- **Waterline:** heavy industrial crust — barnacles, oil sheen, rust-bleed on the
  half-submerged containers and tanker hull.
- **Set-piece (The Gauntlet):** five cranes swinging containers on a timer. The
  motion *is* the spectacle (animated life). Sodium-yellow work-lights make the
  swinging steel read; the beached freighter is the warm-lit landmark.

## 7. Doge's Drift · Drowned ochre · 35 / 35 / 30

Drowned Venice — the most *balanced* track, all three states in tension. Ochre +
terracotta, mossy green at the waterline, Adriatic teal, furnace-orange glow.

- **Built (35):** the palazzi facades, St. Mark's domes, and the Murano
  glassblower furnaces still burning ("Murano keeps blowing glass") — warm,
  alive, the human persistence note.
- **Broken (35):** the half-submerged Doge's Palace, the toppled Campanile, the
  partially-collapsed Rialto arch.
- **Blooming (30):** mossy green at every waterline, ivy on facades, Adriatic
  sea-life in the canals-turned-ocean.
- **Waterline:** the signature — *mossy green* on warm stone, the classic Venice
  acqua-alta tide-mark, everywhere. This track lives at its waterline.
- **Set-piece (The Campanile Fall):** ride up the fallen brick shaft, launch off
  the belfry stub past the swinging bell, gold domes below. The furnace-orange
  glow + gold Byzantine domes are the warm hero accents against teal water.

## 8. Golden Gate Drowned · Urban neon (fog grade) · 45 / 40 / 15

"The city moved uphill and kept the party going." The model track for terrain-
verticality. International Orange, fog white-grey, bay steel-blue, warm low sun.

- **Built (45):** the hill survivors with warm microgrid lights (Nob/Russian/
  Telegraph), the bridge silhouette, Coit/Sutro towers — the dry-land party.
- **Broken (40):** the drowned Financial District, Salesforce + Transamerica
  rising from flooded canyons, the streets-turned-canals.
- **Blooming (15):** kelp + bay life in the flooded grid, hillside green.
- **Waterline:** the drowned skyscraper bases get crust + kelp where the bay
  reclaimed the downtown grid; the hills stay dry above.
- **Signature — the fog:** *Karl* rolls in and peels back on a world timer.
  Render it as **volume**, white-grey, telegraphed not blinding — edge-lights and
  the wave-line stay visible. The fog is the track's identity.
- **Set-piece (The Break):** crest the near-vertical street where the road is
  gone, the drowned city + tower-tops spread below through fog, then splashdown.
  "The frozen wave breaking into the real one" — light the crest warm, the drop
  cool-and-foggy.

## 9. Kilauea Crown · Volcanic outlier · 0 / 20 / 80

The living volcano — its own grade entirely (lava orange-red, black basalt,
steam-white, volcanic-blue lake, lush windward green). "Blooming" here = primal
*creation*, the mountain still building.

- **Built (0):** none — the mountain is the new high ground, untouched by the
  Circuit beyond the racing line.
- **Broken (20):** old lava fields, cooled basalt, weathered rock.
- **Blooming (80):** the dominant — active lava (emissive), the lush windward
  forest, steam plumes, the volcanic-blue lake. Life and fire, not decay.
- **Waterline (the Black Beach):** where lava meets ocean — black basalt sand,
  white steam explosions, orange glow under blue sky. The most dramatic waterline
  in the set; the steam *is* the spray.
- **Set-piece (The Black Beach):** ride alongside the lava waterfall into the
  sea. Lava is the privileged emissive; let it glow under the blue sky and bloom
  the steam. Crescendo lighting at the finish.

## 10. Aqualand · Waterpark outlier · 30 / 45 / 25

"Aqualand closed in '32. The wave generator was on a solar circuit. Nobody turned
it off." Doubly drowned waterpark — *trashy, funny, sun-bleached*. Its own faded-
primary grade. The Circuit thinks this is hilarious; the art should too.

- **Built (30):** the still-running wave generator, the half-pipe slide, lifeguard
  towers (at angles), the PA system still cycling snack-bar ads.
- **Broken (45):** sun-bleached faded plastic, algae everywhere, locker rooms full
  of crab nests, grime over the bright pool tile.
- **Blooming (25):** algae greens, crab/sea-life nests, pool-life in the flooded
  basins.
- **Waterline:** algae + sun-bleach on faded primary plastic — the bright pool-
  tile blue peeking through grime is the signature read.
- **Set-piece (The Tsunami):** the escalating wave-pool surge. The lifeguard
  tower's digital countdown is a warm emissive cue; the bad-Hawaiian-shirt energy
  is the tone. Keep the faded primaries *cheerful*, not bleak.

## 11. Angkor Drowned · Drowned ochre · 0 / 35 / 65

"Angkor outlasted the Khmer Empire... the flood is just the latest thing it'll
outlast." Jungle winning over sandstone — blooming-dominant, no human-built.

- **Built (0):** none living — only the ancient temple stone, which reads as
  *broken* (abandoned), not built.
- **Broken (35):** mossy grey temple stone, the monumental staircases, fallen
  pillars, laterite brick.
- **Blooming (65):** the dominant — deep jungle greens, strangler-fig roots,
  moss everywhere, monkeys, dappled canopy light. Nature reclaiming.
- **Waterline:** moss + fig-root + algae where the flooded inner courtyards meet
  the stone; dappled light through canopy gaps onto wet stone.
- **Set-piece (The Smiling Faces):** sixteen Bayon stone faces in sequence on the
  opening straight. *They watch you go by.* Carve the faces to read as serene
  silhouettes at speed; warm sandstone gold under green canopy shafts.

## 12. Liberty Drowned · Urban neon · 15 / 55 / 30 — *FINALE*

"She fell forward in '71. The Circuit makes her the last lap of every
championship." Broken-heavy reclaim; copper-green oxidation, NYC granite, harbor
steel-blue, **end-of-day finale lighting always**.

- **Built (15):** a few survivor microgrid lights in the Manhattan rooftops —
  the faint human ember in the ruin.
- **Broken (55):** the dominant — the half-submerged Statue, fallen torch arm,
  Trinity spire, the yellow-cab shoal, sagging Brooklyn Bridge cables. The
  Planet-of-the-Apes silhouette.
- **Blooming (30):** copper-green verdigris (life-as-oxidation), barnacle/algae
  waterline at her waist, harbour sea-life over the drowned bull pit.
- **Waterline (at her waist):** the most symbolic shoreline in the set — full
  trio on the copper, granite, and the submerged rooftops. Make it read.
- **Set-piece (The Torch Arm):** ride *up* the fallen copper-green arm to the
  flame, launch off the fist — "the postcard moment of v1." Sunset orange on
  water, copper-green oxidation, her fingers framing the shot. Light it as the
  emotional payoff of the whole game.

## Cross-track dressing rules

A few things every track art pass should honour (from
[art-direction.md](./art-direction.md) + [level-visual-quality-research.md](./level-visual-quality-research.md)):

- **Fill the mid-ground.** The known gap is filler — scatter, debris, signage,
  pilings. JetMoto/Wave Race density, not "terrain + one landmark."
- **Sweep the racing line clean.** The line + ~6 m shoulder reads swept, never a
  forest. The scatter mask already computes this distance.
- **Waterline trio on every shoreline.** It's what makes drowned read *drowned*.
- **Emissive = survivor life at night.** Microgrid windows, neon, furnace, lamp,
  lava — only powered/alive things glow.
- **A few moving things.** Gulls, foliage sway, flags, crowd flats, cranes,
  turbines — animation sells "alive."
- **The set-piece carries the postcard.** Build + light it first; dressing
  supports, never competes.
- **Light explorer touch.** A planted Circuit flag at the set-piece, maybe one
  base-camp cluster near the grid — don't overdo it.
- **Sun + swell bearing rule (wave readability — P1.4,
  [water-next-research.md](./water-next-research.md) §5).** Light direction is
  not a vibe choice: frontal light makes luminance "frequency-double" against
  the swell (two bright cycles per bump — actively misleading), while a **low,
  raking, cross-track sun** maximises how well a rider reads an approaching
  face. On every wave-reading section the grade must satisfy: sun LOW
  (sunset/sunrise band — our default palette already is), and **cross-track or
  over-shoulder relative to the section's travel direction — never dead-ahead
  down a wave straight** (silhouetted faces) and never dead-behind at noon
  height. Both levers are per-track JSON: `sky.timeOfDay` (sun) and
  `water.swellBearingDeg` (swell train; absent = the global 47°). When a
  layout forces a sun-aligned straight, re-aim the *swell* instead of the sun
  — the bearing is the cheaper, grade-neutral lever. Verify in the
  chase-cam `pnpm gen:track-shots` captures: on wave sections you should see
  lit face / shadowed back per swell, not a uniform glare field.

  **Reef Cup audit (2026-06-09, at the time of the P1 pass):** none of the
  trio authors `swellBearingDeg` yet (all ride the global 47°). Mayday Bay
  `timeOfDay 0.0` / Beaufort 1, Mexico City `0.85` / 2.4, Cape Town Drift
  `125.0` / 2 — mixed encodings (normalized vs degrees) that resolve through
  `SkySystem.setTimeOfDay`. Action for the per-track grade passes: check each
  map's main wave-reading straights against the rule in the track-shot
  captures, and author `swellBearingDeg` where a straight runs sun-aligned.
  Playtest verdict (Matt's hands) outranks the captures.
