# Pacer (R8 Games, 2020)

A cautionary tale of spiritual-successor execution: Pacer is the most direct
WipEout heir on the market, made by people who literally built WipEout — and it
still landed with a thud.

## 1. Studio & lineage

- **Developer / publisher**: R8 Games Ltd, a small UK studio. Founder Andrew
  Walker was on the Wip3out team at Psygnosis Leeds and received a BAFTA for
  best design on that title in 1999. Early R8 staff included other ex-Psygnosis
  / Studio Liverpool veterans from the WipEout 3 era.
- **Designers Republic**: tDR — the Sheffield design studio responsible for the
  iconic Wip1/2097/Wip3 visual identity — contributed to Pacer's branding and
  in-game graphic design, giving it the closest "official" tDR feel of any
  WipEout-style game since Sony sunsetted the series.
- **Soundtrack**: Tim Wright a.k.a. **CoLD SToRAGE** — the composer of the
  original WipEout score — wrote new music for Pacer, alongside additional
  tracks from electronic artists. (Note: some sources call out CoH Records /
  CoLD SToRAGE-adjacent labels; the headline name is CoLD SToRAGE.)
- **Release timeline**:
  - April 2015: Kickstarter as **Formula Fusion** (1,830 backers, ~£79,656).
  - August 2015: Steam Early Access.
  - May 2017: 1.0 PC release as Formula Fusion.
  - October 29, 2020: rebranded and relaunched as **Pacer** on PC, PS4, Xbox
    One after a substantial revamp.

This is *the* most direct WipEout successor by lineage.

## 2. Typical lap length / race duration

Pacer doesn't publish official track-length figures, but from career-mode
playthroughs and Steam community discussion:

- **14 tracks** at launch, designed in roughly the WipEout 2097/HD mould.
- **Lap times** typically **40-70 seconds** on standard speed classes; faster
  classes (F1000-equivalent) drop into the **~30-45 s** range — squarely
  WipEout HD/Omega Collection territory.
- **Race length**: standard career races are **3-5 laps**, giving total race
  durations in the **2-4 minute** window. Endurance and Elimination events
  push this longer.
- **Speed classes**: trainee through to F1000 (top tier), mirroring WipEout's
  Vector / Venom / Flash / Rapier / Phantom ladder.

## 3. Race structure & signature mechanics

- **Ships**: anti-grav craft (5 manufacturers at launch), deeply customisable
  via parts for Engine, Handling, Braking, Anti-Gravity and Defence; presets
  for High Speed / Agility / Defensive / Drifter.
- **Weapons economy**: pickup pads on track refill the equipped weapon, exactly
  like WipEout. Players choose a loadout pre-race (defensive, short-range,
  long-range, multi-target) and can mod weapons for ammo, damage, fire rate
  or multi-targeting — more configurable than WipEout's fixed pickups.
- **Handling**: dual airbrakes for cornering, with airbrake use charging boost
  faster (KERS Boost system regenerates over time). Sideshift-style quick
  lateral moves are present.
- **Boost / pads**: boost pads, weapon pads, and shield-recharge pads
  scattered along tracks.
- **Modes**: Race, Time Trial, Elimination (last-place culled every 30 s),
  Endurance, Destruction, **Flowmentum** (Zone-mode analogue: speed ramps up
  through gates), and **Storm** (battle-royale-style shrinking ring).
- **Multiplayer**: up to **10 players** online, 7 modes, in-game VOIP. **No
  split-screen** on any platform.
- **Career**: ~10 race teams / sponsor sets, each with multiple events.

## 4. Differences from WipEout (subtle, because it *is* the team)

- Lower budget and much smaller team than Studio Liverpool's WipEout HD /
  2048 / Omega Collection era.
- More modern, slightly grittier visual style — neon and chrome still, but
  less of the polished pop-tDR sheen of Omega Collection.
- Deeper ship/weapon customisation than WipEout ever offered.
- 10-player online is larger than WipEout HD's 8.
- Different studio (R8 Games, not Sony/Studio Liverpool), no first-party
  polish budget, and shipped without the QA pass a Sony title would have had.

## 5. What players love

- **It feels like WipEout.** The single most repeated line in positive Steam
  reviews and r/wipeout threads.
- **Soundtrack** — CoLD SToRAGE returning is the headline asset, repeatedly
  cited as the strongest single element.
- **Core handling** — airbrakes, cornering, boost economy land well. Multiple
  reviews call the racing itself "tight" and "responsive".
- **Customisation depth** — loadout/parts tuning praised as more flexible than
  WipEout's fixed-ship approach.
- **Visual identity** — tDR-adjacent branding hits the nostalgia button.
- **Bots not too rubber-bandy** — an oddly common compliment given the genre.

## 6. What players criticise (a lot)

Pacer's reception was rough. Steam sits at **"Mixed"** (~72% positive across
~575 reviews), and the negative reviews cluster tightly:

- **Bugs at launch**: UE4 crash-to-desktop, broken control remapping,
  inverted stat changes when swapping parts, locked cosmetic options.
- **Performance**: frame drops from 120 → 30-60 fps at specific track
  sections, stutter on otherwise capable hardware.
- **Content thin vs. WipEout**: 14 tracks / 5 manufacturers feels light next
  to WipEout Omega Collection's 26 tracks / 46 ships — especially given Pacer
  was in dev for 5 years.
- **AI**: hilariously bad at combat in destruction events; difficulty curve
  spikes inconsistently in career.
- **Multiplayer is dead**: the most damaging long-term complaint. Server
  lists are empty within months of launch; reviewers in 2020-2021 could
  not get matches. r/wipeout threads describe MP as "DOA".
- **"Discount WipEout" sentiment**: the comparison cuts both ways — players
  came for WipEout and felt they got 80% of it.
- **No split-screen** anywhere, which is a hot button for couch-co-op fans.
- **Weapon balance** and lacklustre weapon feedback — pickups feel weaker
  than WipEout HD's.

## 7. Most-requested features

Almost all "requests" are **fixes**, not features:

- Stabilise online MP and seed enough players to make it viable.
- Fix the catalogue of launch bugs (control remap, stat inversion, cosmetics).
- Performance pass for high-refresh PCs.
- More tracks / content drops.
- Split-screen for couch play.
- Better AI tuning at higher difficulties.

## 8. Reception numbers

- **Metacritic PC**: **70** (11 critic reviews) — "mixed or average".
- **Metacritic PS4 / Xbox One**: "generally favourable" mid-70s on console
  versions (smaller review counts; console releases had a smoother launch).
- **OpenCritic**: **"Fair"**, average **73**, **46% of critics recommend**,
  37 reviews.
- **Steam**: "Mixed" — ~72% positive across ~575 user reviews.
- **Sales**: R8 Games has not disclosed figures. Indirect signal: the
  Kickstarter had 1,830 backers; CCU on SteamDB after the relaunch peaked in
  the low hundreds and fell into single digits within months — consistent
  with the dead-multiplayer reports.
- **Post-launch support**: very limited; no major content expansions
  followed the 2020 relaunch.

## Takeaway for a hover-bike racer

Pacer proves that **lineage and pedigree are not enough**. The same team, the
same composer, the same design language — and it still failed to recapture
the audience. The lessons it offers:

1. **Ship with launch-day polish or don't ship**. Anti-grav-racer audiences
   are small and unforgiving; one bad month kills the MP scene permanently.
2. **Don't ship thin**. WipEout HD/Omega set the content bar, and 14 tracks
   reads as 50% of expectations for this genre.
3. **Online MP cannot be the headline feature** for a small game — design so
   single-player + couch play carry the value.
4. **The "spiritual successor" framing is a trap**. It invites direct
   comparison with a beloved AAA series, which the player will lose.
